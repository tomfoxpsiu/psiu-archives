Michael Herr

Look up on Wikipedia ↗
  • ChapterPi
  • Class of1961
  • InstitutionSyracuse University
  • FieldEntertainment

Michael Herr was Screenwriter who contributed to several award-winning films. He provided narration for Coppola's Apocalypse Now (1979) and wrote the narration for Francis Ford Coppola's The Rainmaker (1997). Herr also co-wrote the screenplay for Full Metal Jacket (1987) alongside director Stanley Kubrick and author Gustav Hasford. His work on Full Metal Jacket earned him an Academy Award nomination for Best Adapted Screenplay in 1988.
