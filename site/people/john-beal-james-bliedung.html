John Beal (James Bliedung)

Look up on Wikipedia ↗
  • ChapterTau
  • Class of1930
  • InstitutionUniversity of Pennsylvania
  • FieldEntertainment

John Beal was an American actor whose career spanned theater, film, and television. After earning a B.S. in economics from the University of Pennsylvania in 1930, he began acting at a local theater. His film debut came with Another Language (1933), and he went on to play notable roles such as Marius Pontmercy in Les Misérables (1935), as well as leading parts in Alimony (1949) and My Six Convicts (1952). During World War II, Beal served in Special Services and the First Motion Picture Unit, working as both an actor and director for Army Air Forces camp shows and training films. His television career included hosting the game show Freedom Rings in 1953 and appearances in popular series such as Bonanza, Road to Reality, and The Nurses.
