Robert O. Anderson

Wikipedia ↗
  • ChapterOmega
  • Class of1939
  • InstitutionUniversity of Chicago
  • FieldPolitics

American business leader, legendary wildcatter and philanthropist; founded Atlantic Richfield Oil Company. Anderson used his clout to support an array of major cultural organizations including the Los Angeles County Museum of Art and Harper's Magazine. Member of the US Business Hall of Fame.
