Guymon Patrick Casady (born 1969) is an American media executive, producer, and talent manager. He is a founding partner of the production company Entertainment 360 and was an executive producer on several iconic television shows like HBO's Game of Thrones, AMC's The Terror, Netflix's Ripley, and Apple TV +'s Stick. He has been nominated for nine Emmy Awards and won four. Casady is also the originating producer of feature films Steve Jobs (nominated for two Academy Awards), Hope Springs (nominated for a Golden Globe), Office Christmas Party, Deep Water, Bruised, and The Fall Guy.
