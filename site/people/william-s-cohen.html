William S. Cohen

Wikipedia ↗
  • ChapterKappa
  • Class of1962
  • InstitutionBowdoin College
  • FieldPolitics

President Clinton's second-term secretary of defense. The former United States Senator from Maine was the first Republican named to a senior Democratic cabinet post since the 1960s. He was the center of Clinton's efforts to establish a bipartisan defense and foreign policy.
