Edward B. Fiske

Look up on Wikipedia ↗
  • ChapterXi
  • Class of1959
  • InstitutionWesleyan University
  • FieldWriters & Publishers

Edward B. Fiske is a former education editor at the New York Times, a position he held from 1974 to 1987. He's highly regarded in the field of higher education and is best known for his book, The Fiske Guide to Colleges. The book has grown from a single edition in 1982 to a series to an entire media company, and it is currently the best-selling college guide in the United States.
