Herbert Baxter Adams

Wikipedia ↗
  • ChapterGamma
  • Class of1872
  • InstitutionAmherst College
  • FieldEducation

Herbert Baxter Adams was an influential professor of history at Johns Hopkins University.He is credited with bringing the study of history into the realm of the social sciences when it had previously been treated as a branch of literature, and his historical writings introduced scientific methods of investigation that influenced many historians, including Frederick Jackson Turner and John Spencer Bassett. Adams was also elected a member of the American Antiquarian Society in 1881. He was instrumental in founding the American Historical Association in 1884, serving as its secretary until 1900, when he stepped down and was appointed the organization's first vice president.
