Billy Hogan is the current CEO of Liverpool Football Club. He was one of the members of the acquisition team at Fenway Sports Group of Liverpool F.C. in 2010, and he became the team's Chief Commercial Officer in 2012. He became the Managing Director in 2017 and was appointed CEO in 2020.
