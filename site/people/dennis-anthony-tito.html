Dennis Tito is an American engineer and entrepreneur. He earned a B.S. in astronautics and aeronautics from New York University in 1962, followed by an M.S. in engineering science from Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute in 1964. He worked as an aerospace engineer at NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory, where he contributed to the planning and monitoring of the Mariner 4 and Mariner 9 missions to Mars. In 2001, he became the world's first self‑funded "space tourist," spending nearly eight days in orbit as part of ISS EP‑1, a visiting mission to the International Space Station.
