Nelson A. Rockefeller

Wikipedia ↗
  • ChapterZeta
  • Class of1930
  • InstitutionDartmouth College
  • FieldPolitics

Like Averell Harriman, Rockefeller was not content to rest upon his inheritance and rose to make his mark on the United States. He began his career as the head of Rockefeller Center, Inc., which coordinated economic and cultural relations between the Americas. He later went on to seek the governor's seat in New York State. After losing to Averell Harriman in his first attempt, he later was successful. The highlight of his political career came in 1974 when he was named by Gerald Ford to serve as vice president of the United States.
