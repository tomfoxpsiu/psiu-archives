Tom O'Brien

Look up on Wikipedia ↗
  • ChapterPi
  • Class of1983
  • InstitutionSyracuse University
  • FieldBusiness

Tom O'Brien is the President of Weather Group, the parent company of The Weather Channel television network, the streaming platform Local Now, Pattrn (the brand focused on climate and environmental coverage), and The Weather Channel en Español (the nation's first 24/7 Spanish-language free-streaming weather news network.) He also serves as Executive Vice President of Allen Media Group. Prior to leading Weather Group, O'Brien held several executive roles, including President and General Manager of WNBC, NBC's flagship station, and Executive Vice President & Chief Revenue Officer at CNBC. O'Brien graduated at the top of his class from Syracuse University's S.I. Newhouse School of Public Communications and the School of Management.
