John Paul Stevens

Wikipedia ↗
  • ChapterOmega
  • Class of1941
  • InstitutionUniversity of Chicago
  • FieldPolitics

John Paul Stevens is the senior Associate Justice of the Supreme Court of the United States. He obtained a Bachelor of Arts in English from the University of Chicago in 1941; while in college, Stevens also became a member of the Omega chapter of Psi Upsilon. Brother Stevens was appointed to the Supreme Court in 1976 by President Gerald Ford, and is the oldest member of the Court.
