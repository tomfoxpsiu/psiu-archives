Jean Salata

  • Class of1988

  • FieldBusiness

Jean Salata is the founder of Baring Private Equity Asia (BPEA), one of the region's largest private equity firms. He and his wife, Melanie, donated $200 million to establish the Salata Institute for Climate and Sustainability at Harvard University in 2022, and he ranked #10 on Forbes' 2026 list of Hong Kong's 50 Richest.
