Nathan D. Abbott

Wikipedia ↗
  • ChapterBeta
  • Class of1877
  • InstitutionYale University
  • FieldEducation

Nathan Abbott was the co-founder of Stanford Law School and served as its first dean. He Graduated from Yale College with a Bachelor of Arts in 1877 and from Boston University School of Law with a Bachelor of Laws in 1883. After roughly seven years practicing law in Boston and two years teaching at Northwestern University, Abbott joined Stanford University, where he created its Department of Law. Abbott's program admitted students without regard to race or gender, welcoming Hispanic, Chinese, Japanese, and female students. He led Stanford Law School until 1907, after which time he was a member of the law faculty of Columbia University in New York City.
