Richard S. Rust

Wikipedia ↗
  • ChapterXi
  • Class of1841
  • InstitutionWesleyan University
  • FieldEducation

Richard Sutton Rust was an American Methodist preacher, abolitionist, educator, writer, and lecturer. He was principal of the New Hampshire Conference Seminary of the Methodist Episcopal Church (known as the Tilton School today) and helped found Wilberforce University. Rust served as the Wilberforce's first president until 1863. During the American Civil War, Rust helped found the Freedmen's Aid Society, an organization dedicated to providing supplies and housing for teachers to educate freedmen and their children in the South. Rush helped found as many as 30 colleges and institutions, including Rust College, the oldest historically Black college affiliated with the United Methodist Church.
