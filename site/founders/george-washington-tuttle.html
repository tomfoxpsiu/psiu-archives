The Fraternity's own biography of him has not been written yet. What follows is the account in the 1941 Annals, quoted in full. A longer life, drawing on the sources listed further down this page, belongs in the bio column of data/founders.xlsx.

George Washington Tuttle, who went to Union College from Wingate, New York, at the age of sixteen, received his bachelor's degree in 1836. He then studied law and was admitted to the bar, but shortly thereafter began a long and prosperous career as a merchant, first in Ulster County and then in New York City. Retiring in 1868, he lived for many years in Bath, New York. He died in 1903.

— Annals of Psi Upsilon, Part 2, pages 60–62 (1941)
