PSI UPSILON DIGITAL MUSEUM
==========================
Everything in this folder is the museum. Read this page and you will know
what to open.


THE THREE DOCUMENTS TO READ, IN THIS ORDER
------------------------------------------

1.  Psi Upsilon Digital Museum - Recommendations and Instructions.docx
    What has been built, what to do next in what order, and step-by-step
    instructions for every job. Start with section 2, "What to do next".

2.  Filling in the Founders and the Timeline.docx
    The two jobs at the top of that list, both done in Excel, no code.
    This is the one to hand to whoever is writing the founders' biographies.

3.  Publishing the Archive on GitHub.docx
    How to put the museum online at an address you can share.


TO LOOK AT THE MUSEUM ON THIS COMPUTER
--------------------------------------

Double-click        build\preview.bat
Your browser opens it at http://localhost:8000
Close the black window when you are finished.


WHAT IS ALREADY WRITTEN
-----------------------

The seven founders' biographies are written - about 3,500 words in all, read out
of the 1884 Epitome, the 1941 Annals, Fiske's 1895 history and the earliest
numbers of The Diamond. Every claim carries the volume and page it came from.
The timeline holds 184 events, 107 of them written by hand and cited.

Where the founders' own accounts contradict each other - and about 1833 they do,
badly - the pages say so instead of choosing. 84 open questions are listed
across the seven founders, each with a note on where someone could settle it.
Those lists are the work that is left.

The most conspicuous gap: NOT ONE of the seven has a recorded burial place.
Nothing in 190 years of our own publications says where our founders are.


TO CHANGE SOMETHING
-------------------

The founders' pages     ->  open  data\founders.xlsx   in Excel
The timeline            ->  open  data\timeline.xlsx   in Excel

Type into the pale yellow cells. Leave the grey ones alone.
Save the file and CLOSE it.
Then double-click       build\build.bat
That reads your changes and rebuilds the whole museum. Two or three minutes.

Then look at it with preview.bat, and when you are happy, publish it in
GitHub Desktop: write a sentence in the summary box, click "Commit to main",
then "Push origin".


WHAT IS IN EACH FOLDER
----------------------

site\      The website itself. This is the only folder that gets published.
data\      The two spreadsheets, and the text pulled out of all 460 scans.
build\     The scripts that turn data\ into site\. You do not need to read them.
crawl\     Saved copies of the psiu.org pages the document list came from.


THE ONE THING NOT TO DO
-----------------------

Do not edit the .json files in data\ by hand. founders.json and
timeline-core.json are written FROM the spreadsheets every time you build.
Anything you type into them will be overwritten. Use the spreadsheets.


IF YOU GET STUCK
----------------

build.bat prints the reason on the screen and waits for you to press a key.
The commonest two are: Excel is still open (close it), or the "category"
column in the timeline has a word that is not one of the six allowed
(founding, expansion, publications, members, conventions, insignia).

Nothing you do on this computer can break the live site. It only changes when
you press "Push origin" in GitHub Desktop.
