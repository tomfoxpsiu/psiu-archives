PSI UPSILON DIGITAL ARCHIVES - replacement site
================================================

Everything is in this folder. The part you publish is the "site" sub-folder.

READ FIRST
----------
  Publishing-the-Archive-on-GitHub.docx
        Step by step, no programming: how to put this online at an address you
        can share. About an hour, most of it waiting for the upload.

  Psi-Upsilon-Digital-Archives-Recommendations-and-Instructions.docx
        The bigger document: what has been built, what to do next in what
        order, and instructions for every job - adding a scan, a person, a
        recording, an exhibit; fixing the broken links on psiu.org; re-reading
        the scans with better OCR; starting the oral histories.

WHY IT HAS TO BE PUBLISHED SOMEWHERE
------------------------------------
The search index is loaded over the network like any other file, so the site
has to be served over the web. Double-clicking site\index.html in Explorer
shows the design but the search box will not return anything.

Two ways, either is fine:
  * GitHub  - see the document above. Keeps the history, free, public.
  * Netlify - make a free account, choose "Add new site" then "Deploy
              manually", and drag the "site" folder onto the drop area. An
              address in about a minute. Can be password-protected.

WHAT'S IN HERE
--------------
  site\         The website. 647 pages: 460 scanned volumes, 93 people,
                50 chapters, 10 song recordings, the heraldry exhibit.
  data\         The metadata and the extracted text of every volume.
  build\        The scripts that regenerate the site.
  .github\      The instruction that tells GitHub to publish the site folder.
  RIGHTS.md     Who owns what in here.

The PDFs themselves were NOT moved. They stay on psiu.org at their existing
addresses; this site links to them and streams them a page at a time.
